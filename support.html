Help with Digitle, the daily number-deduction puzzle game.

How do I play?

Each day has one secret number. Submit a guess and each digit is colored to give you a clue: green means that digit is correct and in the right position, amber means the digit is somewhere in the number but in a different position, and gray means it isn't in the number at all. You have six guesses to figure out the secret before a new puzzle appears the next day.

Why does the leaderboard say I need to be signed into iCloud?

The leaderboard uses Apple's iCloud service to keep your score in sync across devices. Make sure you're signed into iCloud under Settings on your iPhone, then reopen Digitle and try again.

Can I change my leaderboard name?

Yes — open the Stats tab and tap "Change Name." Your leaderboard entry updates the next time you win a puzzle.
